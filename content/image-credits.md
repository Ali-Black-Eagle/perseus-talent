## 2. Replacing with Commissioned Photography Before Launch

If the business owner commissions professional photography (e.g., from an executive portrait session or clinical provider site):

1. **Format:** WebP, AVIF, or high-quality JPG.
2. **Aspect Ratios:**
   - Dual Audience Cards: **4:3** (minimum 800×600px).
   - Sector Feature / Hero: **3:2** (minimum 1200×800px) or **16:9**.
3. **Colour Treatment:** Consistent warm, slightly desaturated grade (saturation ~85%) with natural light. Avoid unnatural handshakes, clinical surgery close-ups, or generic stock art.
4. **Placement:** Replace files in `public/images/<page>/` maintaining the existing file names, or update the respective page components.
