## Role Summary

We are seeking an experienced and dedicated Senior Clinical Nurse Specialist to join an expanding clinical team in Manchester. In this role, you will lead complex patient assessments, collaborate with multi-disciplinary clinical leads, and champion highest standards of patient care.

### Key Responsibilities

- Lead patient care pathways and clinical assessments.
- Provide guidance and mentorship to junior nursing staff.
- Ensure compliance with CQC guidelines and NHS trust clinical protocols.
- Act as the senior point of escalation for complex patient consultations.
