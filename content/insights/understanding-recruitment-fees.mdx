Recruitment fee structures within professional staffing often vary widely, with tiered scales, sliding bands, and supplemental charges creating unnecessary administrative complexity for hiring managers.

## The Simplicity of a Flat Percentage

Under a flat-rate model, the fee is calculated as a single, consistent percentage of the successful candidate's annual gross base salary. For example, at a 15% flat rate:

- A £40,000 base salary hire incurs a fee of £6,000 (excluding VAT).
- An £80,000 clinical lead appointment incurs a fee of £12,000 (excluding VAT).

Because the rate remains constant, employers can budget hiring costs accurately without worrying about unpredictable tier escalations.

## Base Salary vs Total Remuneration

A transparent fee agreement applies specifically to the contractual gross base salary, excluding discretionary bonuses, pension contributions, and car allowances, ensuring commercial fairness for all parties involved.
