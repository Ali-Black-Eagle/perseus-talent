The success of any executive or specialist recruitment search is established in the initial briefing. A comprehensive brief provides recruiters with the context necessary to identify professionals whose capabilities, leadership style, and working expectations align with your organisation.

## Define Must-Haves vs Desirable Attributes

Distinguishing between non-negotiable statutory requirements (such as professional NMC or HCPC registrations) and adaptable preferences prevents narrowing your candidate pool prematurely.

- Clarify specific clinical competencies required from day one.
- Outline areas where orientation or continued professional development can bridge technical nuances.
- Emphasise interpersonal communication and team dynamics essential for the care setting.

## Be Transparent on Salary and Remuneration

Ambiguous salary descriptions create friction. Specifying the exact gross base salary enables recruiters to engage candidates whose financial expectations match your budget from the outset.
